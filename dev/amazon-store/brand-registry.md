# Amazon Brand Registry enrollment: Mount of Olives

Everything the enrollment form asks for, answered in advance. Enrollment happens at brandregistry.amazon.com (sign in with the Seller Central login), Enroll a brand. Approval usually takes two to ten days; Amazon emails a verification code to the trademark correspondent of record (often the attorney who filed), and the code must be entered in Brand Registry to finish.

## Before starting: confirm these from the trademark record (application serial 97618596)

| Check | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Mark text, exactly as registered | The brand name typed into the form must match it character for character ("Mount of Olives", not "Mount of Olives Anointing Oils"). |
| Status: Registered (registration number) or Pending | A registered mark enrolls directly. A pending application also qualifies when filed with the USPTO, or through Amazon's IP Accelerator. |
| Mark type: standard character (word mark) or design mark | A design mark needs the mark image uploaded with the application. |
| Owner name | Must match the legal entity on the Seller Central account, or the owner has to add the seller as an authorized user after approval. |
| Goods and class | Anointing oils and body oils sit in Class 3; the brand's products on Amazon must fall under the registered goods. |

## The form, field by field

| Field | Answer |
|---|---|
| Brand name | Mount of Olives |
| Trademark office | United States Patent and Trademark Office (USPTO) |
| Registration or serial number | Registration number from the certificate if registered; otherwise application serial 97618596 |
| Mark type | Word mark (standard characters) if the record says so; otherwise Design mark, upload the registered mark image |
| Trademark owner | As listed on the record (must match the seller account's legal name) |
| Your relationship to the brand | Brand owner |
| Do you manufacture the products | Yes: blended and bottled by the brand in Richmond, Texas |
| Do you sell on Amazon | Yes, through the existing seller account |
| Where are products sold | United States (add other countries only if the mark is registered there too) |
| Brand website | https://mountofolives.co |
| Product categories | Beauty & Personal Care (body oils, skin care); Health & Household if any listing sits there |
| Do products carry GTINs or UPCs | Answer from the listings; Amazon asks because branded products normally have them |
| Brand logo | `images/logo-400x400.png` (the round Mount of Olives Anointing Oils mark) |
| Product images showing the brand on the product | At least one, better two, photos where "MOUNT of OLIVES" is readable on the label (see below) |

## Images that show the brand on the product

The label of every 4 oz bottle carries "MOUNT of OLIVES" above the oil name, so the standard product photos qualify. Use the full-size originals in Shopify (Products, open the oil, first image, Download):

- Frankincense & Myrrh 4 oz: `4ozF_M.jpg`
- Hyssop 4 oz: `4ozHyssop.jpg`
- Rose of Sharon 4 oz: `4ozRoseofSharon.jpg`
- Spikenard 4 oz: `4ozSpikenard.jpg`

If Amazon asks for a photo of packaging, use a box photo (the collection images in Shopify, `4_oz_box_3.jpg` and the others) where the name is printed on the carton.

## After approval

1. Stores: Seller Central, Stores, Manage Stores, Create Store. Build from `README.md` and `copy.md` with the images in `images/`.
2. A+ Content on each listing (Advertising, A+ Content Manager): the same claim-free copy and tile images can be reused; Claude can write and submit it through the Selling Partner connector once that is connected.
3. Sponsored Brands ads can point at the Store once it is published.

## If Amazon declines

- "Brand name does not match the trademark": retype it exactly as on the record, including capitalization.
- "Trademark owner does not match": upload a letter of authorization from the owner, or switch the seller account's legal entity to the owner.
- "Images do not show the brand": use photos where the printed name is legible and not cropped; no renders or mock-ups.
- "Verification code not received": ask the attorney of record to forward Amazon's email, or update the correspondent at the USPTO first.
