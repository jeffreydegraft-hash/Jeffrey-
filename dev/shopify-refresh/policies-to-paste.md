## Refund policy (replace the whole policy)

We want you to love your anointing oil. If something is not right, we will make it right.

**Returns.** Unopened, unused oils in their original packaging may be returned within 30 days of delivery. To start a return, email us at info@mountofolives.co with your order number. We will reply with the return address. Return shipping is paid by the customer unless the return is due to our error.

**Opened oils.** Because our oils are personal care items used in prayer and anointing, we cannot accept returns of opened or used bottles.

**Damaged or incorrect orders.** If your order arrives damaged, leaking or incorrect, email us within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We will send a replacement or give you a full refund, at no cost to you.

**Refunds.** Once we receive and inspect your return, we will email you to confirm. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 10 business days. Your bank may take a few more days to post it.

**Exchanges.** To exchange an oil for a different scent or size, return the unopened bottle as described above and place a new order.

**Sale items and gift cards.** Discounted items may be returned on the same terms. Gift cards cannot be returned or refunded.

**Questions.** Email info@mountofolives.co and we will be glad to help.
