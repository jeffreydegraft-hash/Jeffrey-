Frankincense and myrrh, two gifts of the Magi, symbolize prayer, worship, and blessing. Frankincense invites a deeper reverence and awareness of God's presence, while myrrh recalls purification and Christ's sacrifice. This blend is made for deepening moments of worship, prayer, and intercession.

At Mount of Olives, our oils go beyond the traditional use of consecrating objects. They are made to anoint both your surroundings and yourself. Rooted in the anointing traditions of Scripture, every oil carries Polyphenol Alpha (α)™, our complex of polyphenols drawn from biblical botanicals, so that an oil made for prayer, worship, and blessing also nourishes the skin.

How to use

  • For daily use in times of worship, intercession, consecration, prayer for healing, cleansing, and deliverance.
  • As a moisturizer, apply after showering and towel-drying, or at any time of day to pulse points such as the wrist.
  • For a mindful bath, pour a little under warm running water. The oil leaves your skin lightly anointed when you step out.
  • For external use only.
