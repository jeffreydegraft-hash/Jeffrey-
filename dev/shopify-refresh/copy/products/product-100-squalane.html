HYDRATE

From the olive, the tree Scripture names more than any other: the oil that lit the tabernacle (Exodus 27:20) and anointed kings (1 Samuel 16:13). Our 100% olive squalane is the base of every anointing oil we make, offered here on its own.

Squalane is a light, stable oil derived from the olive, and it is vegan and sustainably sourced. It absorbs quickly without feeling greasy, moisturizes, and helps the skin hold on to hydration, leaving it soft and supple. It is non-comedogenic and suited to all skin types. On hair it adds shine and smooths flyaways.

All natural formulation

Natural. Paraben-free. Vegan.

Ingredients: 100% olive-derived squalane

How to use

  • Face: warm a few drops between your hands and press gently into the face, neck, and décolleté.
  • Body: apply a few drops after showering, especially to arms, legs, and knees.
  • Hair: smooth a small amount through dry or damp hair to add shine and tame flyaways.
  • For external use only.
