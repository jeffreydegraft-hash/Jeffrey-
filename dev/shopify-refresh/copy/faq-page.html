Products & Ingredients

What makes Mount of Olives anointing oils different from other anointing oils?
Our oils begin with the botanicals Scripture names, like frankincense, myrrh, spikenard, and hyssop. We extract, purify, and concentrate their naturally occurring polyphenols, terpenes, and other compounds, then blend them into olive-derived squalane, a light base that leaves the skin feeling nourished rather than greasy.

What is Polyphenol Alpha (α)™?
Polyphenol Alpha (α)™ is our proprietary antioxidant-rich complex derived from natural botanical sources. It combines multiple polyphenolic compounds (flavonoids, phenolic acids, stilbenes, and lignans) from biblical botanicals, and it is in every oil we make.

Why do you use squalane as a base instead of olive oil?
Olive-derived squalane is closely related to the squalene naturally present in human skin. It is light, leaves the skin feeling hydrated without feeling greasy, and it's non-comedogenic, so it won't clog pores.

Are your products safe for sensitive skin?
Our formulations are non-comedogenic and free from parabens, phthalates, sulfates, and synthetic fragrances. If you have specific skin concerns, we recommend doing a small patch test first.

Are your products cruelty-free?
Absolutely. We honor all of God's creation and never test on animals.
