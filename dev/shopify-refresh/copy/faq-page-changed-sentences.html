What makes Mount of Olives anointing oils different from other anointing oils?
Our oils are formulated using cosmeceutical science: we extract, purify, and concentrate the naturally occurring polyphenols, terpenes, and bioactive compounds found in biblical botanicals like frankincense, myrrh, spikenard, and hyssop. These are then formulated in olive-derived squalane, which enhances absorption so the active compounds actually penetrate your skin rather than just sitting on the surface.

What is Polyphenol Alpha (α)™?
Polyphenol Alpha (α)™ is our proprietary antioxidant-rich complex derived from natural botanical sources. It combines multiple polyphenolic compounds (flavonoids, phenolic acids, stilbenes, and lignans) that work together to neutralize free radicals, support the skin's barrier function, and promote cellular vitality.

Why do you use squalane as a base instead of olive oil?
Olive-derived squalane is bioidentical to the squalene naturally present in human skin, which means it absorbs efficiently and carries our botanical actives deeper into the skin. It provides superior hydration without feeling greasy, and it's non-comedogenic, so it won't clog pores.
