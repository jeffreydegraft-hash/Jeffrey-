Mount of Olives

At Mount of Olives, we craft premium anointing oils that bring together the wisdom of Scripture and careful, modern craft. Every bottle is formulated by hand in Texas, where we combine carefully sourced biblical botanicals with our proprietary Polyphenol Alpha (α)™ complex to create oils that honor sacred tradition and care for the skin.

Crafted in Texas

Our formulation studio is based in Richmond, Texas, where each anointing oil is carefully blended, tested, and bottled. We believe that what you place on your body, a temple of the Holy Spirit, deserves the highest standards of purity, precision, and care. That commitment to craftsmanship is at the heart of everything we make.

Global Botanicals, Biblical Roots

We source our botanicals from the finest growing regions around the world: the same plants, resins, and extracts that have been revered for millennia. Frankincense from the ancient resin trees of the Middle East and India. Myrrh, spikenard, hyssop, pomegranate, and rose of Sharon: each one handpicked for quality and authenticity. These aren't ordinary ingredients. They carry the weight of Scripture and the richness of nature's own compounds.

Science Meets Scripture

What makes Mount of Olives different is what happens after we source our botanicals. We extract, purify, and concentrate the naturally occurring polyphenols, terpenes, and other compounds from these sacred plants, then blend them into olive-derived squalane. The result is an anointing oil made for prayer, worship, and blessing that also nourishes your skin.
