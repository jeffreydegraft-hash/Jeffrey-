Mount of Olives

At Mount of Olives, we craft premium anointing oils that bring together the wisdom of Scripture and the precision of modern cosmeceutical science. Every bottle is formulated by hand in Texas, where we combine carefully sourced biblical botanicals with our proprietary Polyphenol Alpha (α)™ complex to create oils that honor sacred tradition while delivering real benefits to the skin.

Crafted in Texas

Our formulation studio is based in Richmond, Texas, where each anointing oil is carefully blended, tested, and bottled. We believe that what you place on your body, a temple of the Holy Spirit, deserves the highest standards of purity, precision, and care. That commitment to craftsmanship is at the heart of everything we make.

Global Botanicals, Biblical Roots

We source our botanicals from the finest growing regions around the world: the same plants, resins, and extracts that have been revered for millennia. Frankincense from the ancient resin trees of the Middle East and India. Myrrh, spikenard, hyssop, pomegranate, and rose of Sharon, each one handpicked for quality and authenticity. These aren't ordinary ingredients. They carry the weight of Scripture and the power of nature's most potent bioactive compounds.

Science Meets Scripture

What makes Mount of Olives different is what happens after we source our botanicals. We extract, purify, and concentrate the naturally occurring polyphenols, terpenes, and bioactive compounds from these sacred plants, then formulate them in olive-derived squalane for optimal absorption. The result is an anointing oil that doesn't just sit on the surface. It penetrates, nourishes, and delivers clinically meaningful concentrations of antioxidants and anti-inflammatory compounds to your skin.
