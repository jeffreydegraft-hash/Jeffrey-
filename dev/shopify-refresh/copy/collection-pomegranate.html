The pomegranate is one of the seven species of the Promised Land (Deuteronomy 8:8), woven into the hem of the high priest's robe and carved into the pillars of the temple. Our Pomegranate anointing oil is crafted from Punica granatum in olive oil with Polyphenol Alpha (α)™, our complex of polyphenols from biblical botanicals. Made for consecration, prayer, and worship that gives thanks for God's abundant blessing, it also nourishes the skin.
