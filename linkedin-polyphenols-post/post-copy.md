## Posting notes

- **Carousel vs. single image.** LinkedIn shows PDFs as a swipeable carousel. Carousels tend to earn more dwell time than a single image, which the feed rewards. Use the PDF for the main post and keep `hero-square.png` for a follow-up or a repost a week later.
- **Link placement.** Posts with an external link in the body usually reach fewer people than posts with the link in the first comment. Option A follows that pattern. If you would rather keep the link in the body, Option B is ready as is.
- **First line.** The feed cuts the caption after roughly two lines on mobile, so the hook question sits first in every version.
- **Hashtags.** Three to five is the sweet spot. The two you already use are kept in all versions.
- **Timing.** Weekday mornings, Tuesday to Thursday, tend to perform best for educational content. Reply to early comments within the first hour.
