Food Curiosity04 / 06

Not all evidence weighs the same.

A compound that looks promising in a petri dish has a long way to go before it means anything for the person eating it.

Cells in a dish
In vitro. What it can do, not what it does in you.
Animal studies
Often at doses far above what food provides.
Human observational
Patterns in populations. Association, not cause.
Human clinical trials
The strongest test of whether an effect is real.

A headline rarely says which step a claim came from. A good reference does.

Polyphenols are not one thing.

Thousands of known compounds, grouped into a handful of major families. Four of the biggest:

Flavonoids

The largest family. Tea, berries, onions, cocoa, citrus, apples.
